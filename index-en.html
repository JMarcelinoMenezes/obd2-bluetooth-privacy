Privacy Policy — OBD2 Bluetooth

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the OBD2 Bluetooth app ("the app", "we") handles user information. By using the app, you agree to the terms described here.

Who we are

The app is developed and maintained by JMMENEZES, an individual developer registered on Google Play. For questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact: monteiromenezes@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses and why

Bluetooth

The app requests Bluetooth permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, and, on older Android versions, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) exclusively to list Bluetooth devices already paired with your phone and connect to an OBD-II adapter (ELM327) that you select from the list. The location permission is required by Android itself for this type of Bluetooth connection on older versions — the app never collects, uses, or stores your geographic location.

Vehicle data

Information you register about your vehicles (brand, model, year) and the diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs) read from your vehicle through the OBD-II adapter are stored exclusively on your own device, in a local database. This data is never sent to any server, nor shared with third parties. The app has no connection to any backend server of its own.

The app may read the vehicle's VIN to suggest the brand and year automatically when registering a vehicle — this number is not stored; it is only used at the moment of reading to fill in the form.

Diagnostic history

If you use the Pro version, the app saves a local history of fault readings per vehicle (date and codes found), for the same reason: to help you track your vehicle over time. This history also stays only on your device.

Purchases (Pro version)

The Pro version purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The app never sees or stores your card or payment information — that data is handled directly by Google, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. The app only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made, in order to unlock the corresponding features.

Internet connection

The app only connects to the internet to process the Pro version purchase through Google Play. There are no ads, no usage/analytics collection, and no other type of communication with servers.

When no paired Bluetooth device is found, the app shows an optional link to buy the recommended OBD-II adapter on Mercado Livre. This link only opens your phone's browser when you tap it — the app does not send any of your data to Mercado Livre.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent any personal or vehicle data to third parties. The only exception is the processing of the Pro version purchase, handled by Google Play Billing, which is necessary for that feature to work.

Storage and security

All vehicle and diagnostic data is stored locally on your device, protected by the Android system's own security protections. If you uninstall the app, this data is automatically deleted along with it.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Deleting vehicles individually within the app ("Vehicles" tab), or
  • Uninstalling the app from your phone, which permanently removes all locally saved data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not intentionally collect data from anyone under 13 years old.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated in the future, should the app offer new features that involve data handling different from what is described here. The last updated date always appears at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions, suggestions, or requests related to this policy can be sent to: monteiromenezes@gmail.com.
